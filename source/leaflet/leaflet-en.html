What Makes
INTO Different

Every INTO program is managed on site, so students can focus on their studies and daily life abroad.

  • Official Local GuardianA U.S. citizen who has lived in the area for 30 years serves as INTO’s official guardian.
  • Managed Dormitory LifeStudents share twin rooms, separated by gender, with international-student mentors looking after daily life.
  • Accredited SchoolOur early study-abroad school in Texas is an accredited private school (AdvancED, now Cognia).
  • Parents IncludedParent-accompanied camps offer a Parent Academy (U.S. admissions, visas, student life, scholarships) and 1:1 career counseling.
  • From Camp to UniversityOne team plans the whole path: camp experience, preparation, boarding school and university.

Our local teams in the U.S., the Philippines, Malaysia and Chiang Mai, Thailand welcome students and parents in person.

CAMP

INTO Camps:
Study and Life Training

INTO camps are about learning and life training. Beyond academics, we also look after daily habits and character.

  1. 01
    Real Classes at a U.S. Private SchoolSchooling camp students join regular classes and after-school activities at a private school in Texas.
  2. 02
    An Environment Where English Is a MustStudents live alongside local students. In the Philippines, camps run six hours of native-speaker classes a day under a 24-hour English policy.
  3. 03
    Self-Management HabitsPhone management, set bedtimes and shared meal duties build self-management through community life.
  4. 04
    Finish One English BookStudents read one English book from cover to cover during the U.S. camp.
  5. 05
    Learning Through CultureSmall tours about twice a week, plus seasonal trips (LA in summer and winter, NBA and football games in fall).

U.S. camps accept children from age 5; parents must accompany children in Grade 4 or below. Camp options and seasonal schedules are provided at consultation, and seats are assigned in the order deposits are confirmed.
